# Fixtures pass 3.43: no chip on the airport pickup

Greg said the "Both" chip on the Sunday pickup was wrong. A group chip means the item is for the children, such as their appointment, and taking them along to pick up their mother is no different from taking them to the grocery store. Version 3.42 is archived as story-fixtures-v3.42.md.

## C-11, the ride row in her playbook

Current: "> This Sunday · Pick Charlotte up at Bayview Regional Airport, 6:00pm · Suggested for Edward · [Both] · Change"

Replacement: "> This Sunday · Pick Charlotte up at Bayview Regional Airport, 6:00pm · Suggested for Edward · Change"

## Section 2, after "A child who comes along gets the group chip."

New paragraph: "**An errand that is not for the children carries no chip, even when they ride along.** Picking Charlotte up at the airport is an adult's errand, like a trip to the grocery store, so the row and its page carry no chip, and where the children's coming along matters, the words say so, as in "Leave home with Alexis and Mallory to pick Charlotte up at Bayview Regional Airport". Whether the rule above, for a child who comes along to a sibling's activity, should change to match is open (issue G45)."

Why the older rule stays for now: it covers Mallory riding along to Alexis's therapy and dance across the demo week, and changing it touches C-06b and the Leave sheets, so it is Greg's call rather than a side effect of this pass.
