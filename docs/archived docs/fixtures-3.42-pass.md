# Fixtures pass 3.42: backups on her playbook are a choice for later

Greg saw that Call Ruth and Take both girls on Charlotte's page for the Sunday pickup read as if tapping them would call Ruth on Monday evening, while she is still planning. On her playbook the backups are what Edward does if something goes wrong on Sunday, so they become a choice of which he tries first. Version 3.41 is archived as story-fixtures-v3.41.md.

## C-11, the Sunday pickup's page

Current:

> Backup plans
>
> Plan B · If the flight is late, Ruth stays with Alexis and Mallory, and Edward goes to Bayview Regional Airport alone.
> [Call Ruth]
> Ruth Delgado · 555-0111
>
> Plan C · If the flight is late, Edward takes Alexis and Mallory, and Mallory sleeps on the way home.
> [Take both girls]

Replacement, after a note explaining why nothing on the page happens now:

> Pick Charlotte up at Bayview Regional Airport
> This Sunday · 6:00pm · Bayview Regional Airport
> Owner · Suggested for Edward
>
> Backup plans
> If the flight is late, which should Edward try first?
>
> (●) Plan B · Ruth stays with Alexis and Mallory, and Edward goes to Bayview Regional Airport alone.
> Ruth Delgado · 555-0111
> ( ) Plan C · Edward takes Alexis and Mallory, and Mallory sleeps on the way home.
>
> The other plan stays ready if the first one doesn't work.
> Change the backup plans

Why: the radios choose an order rather than one plan, because Plan C is the backup that depends on nobody else saying yes, and dropping it would leave Edward with nothing if Ruth doesn't answer. There is no main button, because nothing is sent from the page and the choice saves with the playbook; a button such as "Have Edward call Ruth" would read as a message sent now. The owner reads "Suggested for Edward" because nothing is his until he answers, and Ask for help comes off because Change on the playbook already gives a part to someone else. Edward's Leave sheet and his coach card on Sunday keep their real Call Ruth and Take both girls buttons.
