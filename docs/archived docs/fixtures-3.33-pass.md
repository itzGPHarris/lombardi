## The weekend Charlotte is away

*These six moments come the week after the demo week and are a route of their own in the prototype, starting point C, "The weekend Charlotte is away". The nickname in the team is auto pilot, and it never appears on screen. Charlotte is on the board of the Brightpath Reading Foundation, a children's literacy nonprofit, and its board goes away for a weekend offsite at Harlow Lake Lodge from Friday, Oct. 23 to Sunday, Oct. 25. She flies with Larkspur Air from Bayview Regional Airport, 35 minutes from home. While she is away she does not want to hear from MVP, so with the coach she builds a playbook, the part of her plan for those days handed to Edward with everything he needs to run it, and MVP stays quiet until she is home.*

*The route pushes against three locked decisions, and Greg approved each on Nov. 19, 2026 for this journey only. Edward sees the days of her plan she puts in the playbook, which is more than a single request. While the playbook runs, anything new for those days goes to Edward without Charlotte sending it, because she ticked one line on the playbook that says so. Edward has the MVP Coach for those days, because the family plan includes it, so coach cards appear on his Today as well as hers.*
