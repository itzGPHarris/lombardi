# Fixtures 3.24 pass

*MVP (Lombardi) · October 18, 2026 · Applies to story fixtures 3.23 and produces 3.24*

This pass settles where Back sits. Twenty-nine boards drew Back at the top right, because the fixtures said a page shows Back "in place of the monogram", and the monogram sits at the top right of a tab. Greg chose the upper left on October 18, 2026, which is where the spec's title bar already put it and where the newer page boards drew it. Back now sits at the top left of every page, sheet page and composer, the monogram stays at the top right of a tab, and the two never share a screen (issue C49).

Each change below quotes the text in 3.23 and gives its replacement, with the reason. The pass was applied to the full document by script, in this order, and every quoted passage was found exactly once, except changes marked "every occurrence", whose count is given. There are 4 changes.

## 2. Section 2, the monogram and Back

**Why.** Greg chose the upper left for Back on October 18, 2026. "In place of the monogram" led 29 boards to draw Back at the top right (issue C49).

**Current:**

````
On every tab, the parent's monogram sits at the top right of the tab's own screen. Pages opened inside a tab, such as a child page, a school card, an article or a draft, show a Back link in its place.
````

**Replace with:**

````
On every tab, the parent's monogram sits at the top right of the tab's own screen. Pages opened inside a tab, such as a child page, a school card, an article or a draft, show a Back link at the top left instead, and no monogram. Back is always at the top left, on every page and composer, so she always finds the way out in the same place.
````
