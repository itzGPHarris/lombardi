## 1. Remove from every Now that holds a leg

- The line under the time that reads "in 10 min" or "in 2 hr 30 min". The countdown moves into a pill (section 2).
- The line "You leave for Westview" and every line like it. The route replaces it.
- The square checkboxes and the name chips on checklist rows.
- The drive line, such as "10 min drive". Drive and Arrive replace it.
- The next station line at the foot of Now. It moves under the route.

## 2. Draw Now as a boarding pass, top to bottom

1. **The route.** "Home → Westview" in the bold body size, with a small chevron at the right edge of Now on the same line. Directly beneath it, in the small size, the next station, such as "Then Cedar Grove, 2:45".
2. **The time and the status pill, on one line.** The large serif time at the left, such as "2:15 pm". At the right, aligned to the middle of the time, a small rounded pill holding the countdown, such as "in 10 min", outlined. From the leaving time onward the pill reads "Time to leave" and is filled dark with light text.
3. **Three fields in a row.** Each field is a small label over a bold value, at equal widths: "Drive" over "10 min", "Arrive" over "2:25", and "Pickup" over "2:30". Use "Starts" in place of Pickup when the stop is a start time, such as school or therapy. When a child leaves with someone else, the fields are "With" and "Starts". When Arrive falls after the stop's time, draw a small clock icon before its value.
4. **A perforation.** A dashed line across the full width of Now, edge to edge, with space above and below it.
5. **The grouped checklist.** Each child's first name is a bold heading, and that child's items sit beneath it. "Both" heads items that go with both girls, and "Before you leave" heads her own tasks for this trip. Each row is an empty circle at the left followed by the item, and every item starts at the same left edge, so a long item wraps under itself and not under the circle. A ticked row has a filled circle with a check, and its text is dimmed. Do not add chips or counts such as "1 of 2" beside a heading.
6. **The actions.** Remind me and Directions as outlined buttons, side by side.

Now has no border and no shadow, because it is not a card. The perforation is the only line drawn inside it.
